# E4 — Parallel branches and barriers

## Prompt

Task E4 of `IMPLEMENTATION.md` §14: parallel branches and barriers. Done when tests cover a barrier reached, a
branch failure with fail-fast on and off, and a deadline during a barrier.

## Summary

- `models/scenario.py`: the `parallel` step gains an optional `timeout`, the barrier's deadline, and the descriptions
  of `fail_fast` and the barrier. The scenario schema is regenerated.
- `scenarios/engine.py`: the engine runs the branches of a parallel step at the same time (`_parallel`, `_branch`).
  Every step runs through `_execute`, which records its verdict under its label when it ends; a step still open when
  a deadline passes is recorded by that deadline (`_end_open`). The run's cancel event halts the branches through
  `_relay`. Each step has its own `Context`.
- D-029 records the decisions: the barrier, the labels, fail-fast, the barrier deadline, and the choice of
  `asyncio.wait` over `gather`.

Dependencies: none new.

## Verification

- `tests/unit/test_scenario_parallel.py` (7 tests): the barrier waits for every branch and the branches start
  together; a failed branch step under fail-fast stops the other branches and the later steps; without fail-fast every
  branch runs to its end; a deadline during the barrier ends the branches still running and says how many; the global
  deadline records each open step; a cancellation halts every branch and runs the cleanup; a parallel step inside a
  branch has its own barrier.
- The engine tests (`test_scenario_engine.py`, `test_scenario_assertions.py`) pass unchanged.
- The three engine test files ran three times with warnings as errors: 50 pass each time.
- Gates: `ruff format --check`, `ruff check`, `mypy` (strict, 58 source files), the unit suite (264 pass), and the
  schema check (`--check`). The runtime scenario test and the milestone test (run against the runtime built by
  `cargo test`) pass.
- Found while testing: a `gather` barrier answers a cancellation only after every branch has ended, so the count of
  branches still running was always zero at a deadline. Fixed with `asyncio.wait`.

## Decisions

- D-029: the parallel step is the barrier; its verdict; the labels; fail-fast halts the other branches; the barrier
  deadline is the optional `timeout`; the open steps at a deadline.

## Points for the next tasks

- E5 (restart and reconnect) follows: it needs the instance actions of §8 (`stop_instance`, `start_instance`), which
  have no handler yet, and it depends on D2 and U2 as well as E1.
- The verdicts of a parallel step are in the order they end, not in the order of their labels; the labels give the
  position. A report (R2) may want to sort them.
- Runs in the cleanup can be parallel, but nothing uses that yet.

## Size

The engine is 493 lines (it held about 340 before, for E1 to E3; E4 adds about 150), the new tests are 290 lines,
and the model and schema changes are small. The change is above the roughly 400 lines of §2 once the tests are
counted: the loop changes as a whole (each step goes through one path, `_execute`), and splitting it would leave two
paths for the same loop.
